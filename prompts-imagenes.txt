PROMPT MAESTRO (colores + dirección general)
==================================================

STYLE — GREAT HEAD, a Japanese-style HEAD SPA in Miami. One editorial photography campaign, same photographer, same day. Single photograph, one frame only.

THE SETTING: the client lies FULLY RECLINED, almost flat, on a padded treatment bed, head resting back into a shampoo basin. The therapist stands behind the head. This is NOT a hair salon: no upright styling chair, no mirror station, no scissors, no brushes, no blow-dryers, no cutting, no styling, no product shelves.

DIRECTION: premium without being precious, fun without being childish, credible without being clinical. Confident, energetic, slightly irreverent, culturally current, like a bold fashion or lifestyle campaign, NOT like a spa. BRIGHT, not moody: hard direct daylight or one hard studio flash, crisp shadows, punchy flat color, no dim ambient gloom, no velvet darkness, no mist-and-candle atmosphere. Walls are FLAT, smooth, matte painted color blocks (a whole wall solid burnt orange, another solid burgundy), never textured plaster, never mottled, never draped. Medium-format editorial look, 80mm, gentle film grain, true skin tones, no HDR, no glow filters. Graphic, calm compositions with generous negative space for text. The arch of the water device, the round basin and round lights repeat a circle motif.

WARDROBE (strict): the client is always dressed. A soft lavender or cream cape or garment covers shoulders and chest up to the collarbone. No bare backs, no bare shoulders, no towels as the only clothing, nothing sensual.

COLORS (strict, and every frame must contain at least one large block of orange or burgundy): burnt orange #E54919 as a strong presence, a full painted wall, the treatment bed upholstery or a large towel; deep burgundy #411922 for a dark wall, floor, leather or shadows; soft lavender #C1C1E7 for the basin, tiles or the therapist's garment; warm cream #F0E4DB only as a secondary surface, never the whole scene; olive green #79701E as a tiny note if at all. No beige-only rooms, no white rooms, no teal, no neon, no purple LED light, no pink, no gold, no blue color grading.

AVOID: hair salon of any kind; beige or white spa rooms; dark moody spa rooms, textured or mottled plaster walls, curtains, drapes, dim mood lighting; nudity or bare skin below the neck; lotus, candles, stacked white towels, bamboo, stones, white bathrobes, orchids, flowers, spa cliches; palm trees, flamingos, art deco, neon, Miami postcard cliches; kanji, torii, cherry blossoms, zen symbols; gloves, lab coats, clinical light; smiley faces; heavy makeup; cartoon or 3D render look; overly feminine styling; collage, split screen, multiple panels, grid, diptych, contact sheet; text, logos, signage, watermarks.

OUTPUT: one single photorealistic editorial photograph, print quality.

DESCRIPCIÓN DEL HALO (pegar siempre después del maestro)
==================================================

THE WATER ARCH DEVICE (exact shape, orientation and motion, do not reinvent, match the attached reference photos):
SHAPE: a single thin ROUND metal tube, about 2 cm in diameter, bent into a smooth half-circle arch roughly 60 cm wide, like a horseshoe standing upright. Not a flat band, not a wide bar. Its two ends are hinged onto the left and right rims of the shampoo basin, just behind the client's head.
ORIENTATION (critical): the client lies on their back with the head resting in the basin. The arch stands BEHIND the crown of the head, its plane perpendicular to the body, so seen from the foot of the basin it frames the top of the head like a halo. Its jets point only at the SCALP and HAIR from behind and above; they never touch the face. The face stays dry and calm.
WATER: along the inside edge of the tube about 30 tiny nozzles shoot thin jets of warm water inward toward the crown, converging like the spokes of a wheel. The jets are lively spray, not rigid straight lines: they wobble, break into droplets, bounce off the hair, catch the light. No shower head, no hose, no single pouring stream, no closed ring, no arm from the ceiling.
MOTION (critical): the arch is motorized and slowly rocks back and forth on its two hinges, sweeping the fan of jets across the whole scalp from the hairline to the nape, like a slow windshield wiper. In a photograph this reads as a slight motion blur on the arch, jets fanning at an angle, droplets flung sideways, mist and steam. It must look alive, not static.
BASIN: a shallow shampoo basin with a padded neck rest, water draining, not a deep bowl of standing water. Round-edged, matte lavender ceramic.
MATERIAL for Great Head: matte burnt-orange powder-coated metal tube (alternatively brushed chrome).
ABSOLUTELY NO gloves on the therapist, no red or purple color cast, no water on the face.
